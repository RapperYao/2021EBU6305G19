There are several famous categories in West Lake Longjing, such as "Lion Peak Longjing", "Meiwu Longjing", "Yunqi Longjing" and "Tiger Running Longjing". "Lion Peak Longjing" produced in Longjing Village, Lion Peak, Wengjiashan area, which is slightly yellow, known as "brown rice color". "Meiwu Longjing" produced in yunqi, Meijiawu area, which has straight appearance, flat smooth and bright green color. There are more than 30 brands of West Lake Longjing Tea.
West Lake Longjing tea is flat and straight in appearance, green in color, fragrant and mellow in inner quality, when soaked in the cup, it is green in bud and leaf. Known as "color green, fragrant, sweet, beautiful shape" four absolutely famous.In spring tea, the super grade West Lake Longjing and Zhejiang Longjing are flat and smooth in shape, with spiky seedlings, longer buds than leaves, bright green in color and no hairs on the body surface. Soup light green (yellow) bright; Fresh or tender chestnut aroma, but some tea with high fire aroma; Refreshing or rich flavor; Pale green at the bottom, still intact.

With the decline of other levels of Longjing tea, the shape and color of the tea changes from light green to green to dark green, the tea body changes from small to large, and the tea strips change from smooth to rough. The fragrance changed from tender to thick, grade four tea began to taste rough; Leaf bottom turns from bud to leaf, color changes from light yellow → green → yellow brown.

Longjing tea in summer and autumn is dark green or dark green in color. The tea body is large and the surface of the tea is not hairy. The liquor color is yellow and bright, with faint fragrance but rough taste and slightly astringent taste.
