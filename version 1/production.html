Pluck

1. Farmers often say: tea early pick three days is a treasure.
2. The picking of Longjing tea is also famous for its tender picking, which constitutes the basis of the quality of Longjing tea.
3. Picking many times is also a major feature of Longjing tea.

Drying

After picking, West Lake Longjing should be dried on the bamboo sieve, which generally takes half a day, so as to reduce the taste of green tea, make the moisture meet the requirements of roasting, and prevent the new tea from clustering during roasting.

Knead

Kneading is the internal structure of the tea by external forces rolling into the shape people want, but the shape of Longjing tea requires the green leaves to retain part of the natural rigidity, so that the tea can also see part of the original shape of the green leaves, so the rolling process weakened.

Fried

1. Fry tea leaves until they are 70 to 80 percent dry.
2. Spread tea leaves flat in the bamboo sieve for moisture recovery.
3. Add tea leaves to the pot and fried dry.
