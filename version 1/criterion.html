The quality of fresh tea leaves of West Lake Longjing Tea can be divided into five grades

01

Special grade

One bud and one leaf are newly developed, the bud and leaf clamp angle is small, the bud and leaf are longer than the leaf, the bud and leaf are uniform and fat, the length of bud and leaf is not more than 2.5cm.

02

First grade

The initial development of one bud and one leaf to one bud and two leaves, mainly one bud and one leaf, the initial development of one bud and two leaves is less than 10%, the bud is slightly longer than the leaf, the bud and leaf are complete and uniform, the length of the bud and leaf is not more than 3cm.

03

Second grade

One bud and one leaf to one bud and two leaves, one bud and two leaves less than 30%, the length of bud and leaf is basically equal, the length of bud and leaf is complete, and the length of bud and leaf is not more than 3.5cm.

04

Third grade

One bud and two leaves to one bud and three leaves, mainly one bud and two leaves, less than 30% three leaves of one bud, longer than the bud, complete bud leaves, the length of bud leaves is not more than 4cm.

05

Fourth grade

One bud with two leaves to one bud with three leaves, one bud with three leaves not more than 50%, leaves longer than buds, with partially tender opposite leaves, length not more than 4.5 cm.
