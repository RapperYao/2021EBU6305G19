Growing Region
There are several famous categories in West Lake Longjing, such as "Shifeng Longjing", "Meiwu Longjing", "Yunqi Longjing" and "Hupao Longjing". "Shifeng Longjing" produced in Longjing Village, Lion Peak, which is known as "brown rice color". "Meiwu Longjing" produced in yunqi, Meijiawu area,which has a straight and flat appearance and a bright green color. There are more than 30 brands of West Lake Longjing Tea.
West Lake Longjing tea is famous for four fabulous features, which are " green color, fragrant smell, sweet taste and beautiful shape". The premium spring West Lake Longjing has spiky seedlings, longer buds than leaves and no fluff on the body surface. When spoiled, the color of the tea is bright and the smell is fresh. With the decline of the levels of Longjing tea, the color turns to darker, the size turns to bigger, the surface turns to rougher, the taste turns to richer, the leaf bottom turns from bud to leaf.

Longjing tea in summer and autumn is dark green and large, there is no fluff on the surface, the taste is rougher than the spring tea and slightly astringent.
The West Lake Longjing is native to the mountains in Zhejiang Hangzhou West Lake, where has a mild climate, abundant rainfall and full light. The soil here is fertile and slightly acidic with good drainage, luxuriant forests and streams. The annual average temperature is 16 ℃ and the annual precipitation is about 1500 mm. The superior natural conditions in favour of the growth of the tea trees and the germination of the buds. The picking time is around 30 batches of all year, which is the most in all types of tea.
The growing region of West Lake Longjing is divided into primary region and secondary region. Primary region including "Lion, Dragon, Cloud, Tiger, Plum" five core regions, secondary region is in addition to the primary region. "Lion" is produced near the Shifeng area, "Dragon" is produced near the Longjing area, “Cloud” is produced near the Yunqi area, “Tiger” is produced near the Hupao area, “Plum” is produced near the Meijiawu area.
